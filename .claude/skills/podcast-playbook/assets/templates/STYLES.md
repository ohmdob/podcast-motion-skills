Status is one of: Not tried · Test frames · Approved · Ruled out. A look joins the question once the producer approves it.

## Rules for every look

- **Faces stay natural.** No halftone, illustration or other treatment that stylises a face.
- **Show it before building it.** Try a new look on real frames before any long render.
- **Graphics only draw the guest's words.** No added figures. Sensitive claims need a qualified person's sign-off before publishing.
- **Names and logos stay unconfirmed until answered.** No name goes on screen until the producer confirms it. Never invent a logo.
- **Type stays live.** Text never goes in a generated image.
- **Avoid** dark voids, glow, and cream paper with serifs.

## Trying a new look

1. Choose real frames from each take. A camera that moved between takes means framing doesn't carry across them.
2. Make test frames at 16:9 and 9:16 (`--frames` on any build), and read them at phone size.
3. Show the frames to the producer. Don't start a long render until a look is chosen.
4. Update this file either way, including the reason if a look is rejected.
